## Colorado Credentialing Guide

### MEDICAID CREDENTIALING (Health First Colorado)

Colorado Medicaid is administered through **Health First Colorado** and managed by Regional Accountable Entities (RAEs).

#### Step 1: Obtain NPI Numbers
- **Type 1 NPI** (Individual): FREE — [nppes.cms.hhs.gov](https://nppes.cms.hhs.gov)
- **Type 2 NPI** (Organization, if forming a group practice): FREE

#### Step 2: Enroll with Colorado HCPF (Healthcare Policy & Financing)
**Website:** [colorado.gov/hcpf](https://www.colorado.gov/hcpf)

**Process:**
1. Complete Colorado Medical Assistance Provider Enrollment Application
2. Submit via the Provider Enrollment Portal: [colorado.gov/pacific/hcpf/provider-enrollment](https://www.colorado.gov/pacific/hcpf/provider-enrollment)
3. Processing time: 30-90 days

**Costs:**
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Medicaid Enrollment | FREE |
| Background Check (CBI) | $39.50 |
| FBI Fingerprint Check | $13.50 |
| **Total Medicaid Credentialing** | **~$53** |

#### Step 3: Enroll with Regional Accountable Entities (RAEs)
Colorado has 7 RAEs managing behavioral health. You must enroll with the RAE(s) in your service area:

| Region | RAE Name | Contact |
|--------|----------|---------|
| 1 | Rocky Mountain Health Plans | rmhp.org |
| 2 | Northeast Health Partners | northeasthealthpartners.org |
| 3 | Colorado Access | coaccess.com |
| 4 | Health Colorado, Inc. | healthcoloradorae.com |
| 5 | Colorado Access | coaccess.com |
| 6 | Colorado Community Health Alliance | cloradocommuntiyhealthalliance.org |
| 7 | Colorado Community Health Alliance | coloradohealthalliance.org |

**RAE Enrollment: FREE** (but requires separate applications)
